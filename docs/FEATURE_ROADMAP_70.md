## Advanced differentiators
- Auction rivals adapt to the manager's bidding habits.
- A persistent cricket-world history affects records, rivalries, and reputation.
- Fictional player storylines and milestones create career narratives.
- AI explains selection/tactical decisions and match outcomes.
- Owner, board, and fan goals create management trade-offs.

## Rule/data cautions
IPL rules vary by season. Keep the ruleset explicit; verify purse, squad cap, overseas limit, retention, and substitute mechanics. Source real-player data responsibly and label generated players as fictional. Prioritize reliable core gameplay and save safety before ambitious additions.
